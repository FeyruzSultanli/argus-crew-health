## What it is

Minutes of countermeasure exercise you did in the day, compared with your plan.

## Why it matters in space

Bones lose about 1–1.5 % of their density per month in flight [SRC_BONE_NASA]. ISS crew average about two hours of exercise a day to protect bone, muscle and fitness [SRC_EXERCISE_2H]. Bone changes and reduced fitness are NASA human-research risks [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

## How to measure

- Log the minutes of each exercise session.
- Count active exercise time, not set-up.

## How ARGUS judges it

- **Space:** your adherence over the last 7 days is your minutes divided by your planned minutes. Watch below 70 % of plan; Act below 40 % [SRC_DEMO_RULE_BANDS].
- **Your plan:** each simulated crew member has a daily exercise plan [SRC_DEMO_SIMULATION], in line with the roughly two hours a day ISS crew average [SRC_EXERCISE_2H].
- **Earth:** no Earth rule is used.
- **Personal:** not used for this signal.

## What to do

- **Watch:** "Re-measure. If it is still outside your usual range, tell the CMO." [demo wording]
- **Act:** "Tell the CMO now and send a report to the flight surgeon." [demo wording]
