## What it is

Altered gravity is one of the five hazards of human spaceflight; Mars surface gravity is three-eighths of Earth's [SRC_NASA_HAZARDS]. NASA's human-research risks include cardiovascular and sensorimotor changes, bone changes, reduced fitness and SANS, the eye changes of long missions [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

In weightlessness body fluid shifts toward the head [SRC_NASA_RISKS]. Bones lose about 1–1.5 % of their density per month on long missions [SRC_BONE_NASA].

## What ARGUS watches

- [Resting heart rate](heart-rate) and [Blood pressure](blood-pressure): circulation adapts to weightlessness.
- [Body mass](body-mass) and [Exercise minutes](exercise): countermeasure exercise protects bone and muscle.
- [Amsler grid](amsler-grid) and [Near visual acuity](near-acuity): screening for SANS, including the known far-sighted shift [SRC_SANS_EYE_NATURE].
- [Face fluid-shift index](face-fluid-index): experimental — not validated; a trend only.
- [Hematocrit](hematocrit): known to read higher in flight [SRC_HCT_FLIGHT].
