## What it is

A hostile, closed environment is one of the five hazards of human spaceflight [SRC_NASA_HAZARDS]. NASA's human-research risks include hypoxia, inadequate food and nutrition, immune changes and host–microorganism interactions [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

On the ISS the Environmental Health System of the Crew Health Care System monitors air, microbes, water, acoustics and radiation [SRC_CHECS_GUIDE]. NASA-STD-3001 sets limits for cabin CO₂ [SRC_STD3001_CO2], temperature, humidity, water and food [SRC_STD3001_ECLSS].
