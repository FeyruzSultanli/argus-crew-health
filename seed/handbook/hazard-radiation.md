## What it is

Space radiation is one of the five hazards of human spaceflight mapped by NASA's Human Research Program, and NASA describes it as one of the most hazardous aspects of spaceflight [SRC_NASA_HAZARDS]. Radiation-induced cancer is one of NASA's human-research risks [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

On the ISS, neutrons contribute about 30 % of the total radiation [SRC_CSA_RADIATION]. On Artemis II each crew member carried a Crew Active Dosimeter, and the Orion vehicle has thousands of radiation sensors [SRC_ARCHER_GIZMODO].

## What ARGUS watches

- [Radiation dose](radiation-dose): your dose ledger against the NASA-STD-3001 career limit of 600 mSv and the solar-event design limit of 250 mSv [SRC_STD3001_RAD].
- Live solar radiation storms from NOAA proton data, on the S-scale [SRC_NOAA_SWPC_PROTONS], and the shelter advisory. See [Radiation and space weather](about-radiation-space-weather) and the [SPE shelter](protocol-spe-shelter) protocol.
