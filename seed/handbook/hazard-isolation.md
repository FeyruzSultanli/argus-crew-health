## What it is

Isolation and confinement are one of the five hazards of human spaceflight [SRC_NASA_HAZARDS]. NASA's human-research risks include behavioural changes, teamwork, and inadequate sleep and irregular schedules [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

NASA's behavioural-health standard measures include a neurocognitive test battery, actigraphy (sleep and activity from a wrist device) and mood self-reports [SRC_BHP_SM]. The battery includes a validated 3-minute psychomotor vigilance test [SRC_COGNITION].

## What ARGUS watches

- [Sleep duration](sleep): the average of the last three nights; some short sleep in flight is a known change.
- [Mood](mood): a daily self-rating; your note goes only to the CMO and is never scanned.
- [Reaction Self-Test](reaction-self-test): response speed and lapses against your own pre-flight runs.
- Short sleep together with a slower Reaction Self-Test suggests the [Sleep debt](protocol-sleep-debt) protocol.
