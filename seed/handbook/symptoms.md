## What it is

A checklist of how you feel today: headache, congestion, nausea or motion sickness, rash, back pain, vision change, insomnia, low mood, fatigue.

## Why it matters in space

Symptoms put the numbers in context: for example a headache together with high cabin CO₂, or a rash or fever with a raised heart rate, links to a protocol. Spaceflight stress can reactivate dormant viruses that cause rashes [SRC_NASA_RISKS].

## How to measure

- Tick every symptom you notice today.
- Leave the list empty if there are none.

## How ARGUS judges it

- Symptoms get no verdict of their own; they combine with other signals to suggest a protocol.
- Headache together with a cabin CO₂ 7-day average of 2.5 mmHg or more suggests the CO₂ headache protocol [SRC_NASA_CO2_PAGE].
- Rash, or a raised resting heart rate on your personal baseline together with a raised temperature, suggests the Suspected infection protocol.
- A vision change asks for the Amsler grid and acuity test now.
- Any note you add is stored and shown to the CMO, nothing else: ARGUS does not search it for words.
