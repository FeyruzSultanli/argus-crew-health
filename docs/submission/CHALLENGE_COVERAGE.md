# Challenge coverage — what the challenge asks for, and where ARGUS shows it

Challenge: *Create Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts on Space Missions* — "a visual tool that helps astronauts
assess their own health during space missions and provides guidance on treatments and health improvements"
(docs/01 §1). Items below are the checklist of docs/01 §6, judged strictly against the build on `main`.

Status: **built** = works in the demo today · **partly** = part of the item works, the rest is labeled or missing ·
**not built** = designed or described only.

## Short form for slide 7 / the Relevance criterion

Built: indicators (BP, HR, glucose mg/dL, temperature, sleep, mood), keyboard + mouse + camera input, verdict and
action guidance, clickable 3D body, animated mission timeline, six mission environments, routine prompts,
gamification, charts with personal bands, colour coding, instructions and an offline handbook, radiation and cabin
awareness, vision check (Amsler grid and near acuity; screening only), face fluid-shift scan (experimental, trend
only). Not built: device import.
